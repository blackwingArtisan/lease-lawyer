LEASE AGREEMENT: Hollowmere Rentals and Iris Penhallow

1. THE PARTIES. Hollowmere Rentals ("Management") leases the dwelling at 9 Placeholder Court, Lakeview, to Iris Penhallow ("Occupant").

2. LEASE PERIOD. The lease period commences on August 15, 2027 and terminates on August 14, 2028.

3. MONTHLY PAYMENT. The rent for the dwelling is $2,050 a month, payable on the first.

4. LATE CHARGES. A late charge of $80 applies to any rent received more than five (5) days after it is due.

5. DEPOSIT. Occupant has given Management a security deposit of $2,050. Management will return the deposit within 21 days of move-out, less the cost of repairing damage, normal wear and tear excepted.

6. ANIMALS. Occupant may keep one cat or small dog with Management's written approval.

7. MANAGEMENT ENTRY. Management may enter the dwelling with twelve (12) hours' notice, or at any time in an emergency.

8. SUBLETTING. Occupant shall not sublet the dwelling or any part of it under any circumstances.

9. EARLY MOVE-OUT. If Occupant receives military orders or a job transfer of more than 50 miles, Occupant may end this lease early on sixty (60) days' written notice without penalty.

10. REPAIRS. Occupant gives up any right to make repairs and deduct their cost from the rent.

11. RENT ADJUSTMENT. Management may increase the monthly rent by 3.5 percent on each anniversary of the lease period.
