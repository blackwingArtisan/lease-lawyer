APARTMENT RENTAL AGREEMENT: Thornbury Property Group and Milo Vance

1. PARTIES. Thornbury Property Group ("Lessor") rents Apartment 12 at 48 Sample Street, Rivertown, to Milo Vance ("Lessee").

2. DURATION. This Agreement runs for twelve (12) months, from June 1, 2027 through May 31, 2028.

3. PAYMENT OF RENT. Lessee agrees to pay Lessor the sum of $1,390 each month in advance.

4. LATE RENT. Rent paid after the third (3rd) of the month incurs a $45 late fee.

5. SECURITY. Upon signing, Lessee shall deposit $1,390 with Lessor as security. Lessor may apply the security only to unpaid rent and to damage in excess of normal wear and tear.

6. PET POLICY. Lessee shall keep no dog, cat, bird or other animal on the premises.

7. LESSOR'S RIGHT OF ACCESS. Lessor and its agents may access the apartment for inspection or repair on forty-eight (48) hours' prior written notice to Lessee.

8. SUBLEASING. Any sublease or transfer of Lessee's interest requires the prior written consent of Lessor.

9. EARLY TERMINATION. Should Lessee need to break this Agreement, Lessee may do so with thirty (30) days' written notice and payment of one (1) month's rent as a termination fee.

10. RENEWAL. If renewed, monthly rent will go up 2% for the renewal year.
