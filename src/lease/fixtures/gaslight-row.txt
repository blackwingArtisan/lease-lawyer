TOWNHOME LEASE
Gaslight Row Townhomes

1. PARTIES. This Lease is entered into by Lanternfield Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Riley Okafor ("Tenant").

2. PREMISES. Landlord rents to Tenant the townhome at 77 Gaslight Row, Hollow Creek, including the attached one-car garage.

3. TERM. This Lease begins on June 1, 2026 and ends on May 31, 2027.

4. RENT. Monthly rent is $2,400.00, payable on the first (1st) day of each month. Beginning with the seventh (7th) month of the term, monthly rent shall increase by three percent (3%).

5. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received within three (3) days after the due date, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $100.00.

6. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,600.00 before move-in. At move-out, Landlord will deduct the cost of professional carpet cleaning and full interior repainting from the deposit, including charges for normal wear and tear.

7. PETS. Pets are permitted, subject to a pet deposit of $500.00 per animal. Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by an animal.

8. LANDLORD'S ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises for inspections or repairs after giving Tenant twelve (12) hours' notice.

9. REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and shall replace light bulbs and air filters. Tenant waives any right to make repairs and deduct the cost from rent.

10. SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not sublet any part of the Premises or list it for short-term rental.

11. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may terminate this Lease early for any reason by giving thirty (30) days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent.

12. HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION. Tenant shall follow the rules of the Gaslight Row Homeowners Association, including the rules on trash bins and exterior decorations.

13. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord shall be sent to gaslight.homes@example.com or by telephone to 555-0187.
