APARTMENT RENTAL AGREEMENT
Cobweb Court

1. PARTIES. This Rental Agreement is between Mortlake Residential LLC, the Landlord, and Casey Thornbury, the Tenant.

4. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,450.00, due on the first (1st) day of each month.

7. ACCESS. Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times with reasonable notice.

8. MAINTENANCE. Landlord shall maintain the Premises in a safe and habitable condition. Tenant shall not paint or alter the Premises.

9. QUIET HOURS. Quiet hours are from 10 in the evening until 7 in the morning every day.

10. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord shall be sent to cobweb.court@example.com or by telephone to 555-0119.
