RESIDENTIAL LEASE
Hollowmere Flats

1. PARTIES. This Lease is made between Gravesend Property Partners LLC, the Landlord, and Jordan Vance, the Tenant.

2. TERM. This Lease begins on August 1, 2026 and ends on July 31, 2027.

3. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,750.00, payable on the first (1st) day of each month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,750.00 before move-in. What may be deducted from the deposit is set out in Exhibit B.

5. ANIMALS. Pets are not prohibited, except as provided in Rider B attached to this Lease.

6. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises at any time to inspect them.

7. SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not fail to obtain Landlord's written consent before subletting any part of the Premises.

8. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received within five (5) days after the due date, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $50.00.

9. REPAIRS. Landlord shall keep the heating, plumbing and electrical systems in good working order.
