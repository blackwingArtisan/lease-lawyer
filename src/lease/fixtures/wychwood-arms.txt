RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
Wychwood Arms Apartments

1. PARTIES. This Residential Lease Agreement is made between Ravenmoor Property Group LLC, as Landlord, and Morgan Ellis, as Tenant. Each Tenant named in this Lease is jointly and severally responsible for all of its terms.

2. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the apartment located at 1428 Wychwood Lane, Unit 3B, Ravenmoor, together with one assigned parking space and the use of the shared laundry room.

3. TERM. The term of this Lease is twelve (12) months, beginning March 1, 2026 and ending February 28, 2027. At the end of the term this Lease ends unless both parties sign a renewal.

4. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,850.00, due on the first (1st) day of each month. Rent shall be paid through the resident portal or by check delivered to the leasing office. Rent shall not change during the term of this Lease.

5. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received within five (5) days after the due date, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $75.00. A charge of $35.00 applies to any returned payment.

6. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing, Tenant shall pay Landlord $1,850.00 as a security deposit. Landlord may deduct from the deposit only unpaid rent and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear. Landlord shall return the deposit, less lawful deductions and with an itemized statement, within twenty-one (21) days after Tenant vacates.

7. USE AND OCCUPANCY. The Premises shall be used only as a private residence for Tenant and the persons listed on the rental application. Guests may not stay longer than fourteen (14) consecutive nights without Landlord's written approval.

8. PETS. No pets of any kind shall be kept on the Premises. Aquarium fish in a tank of twenty (20) gallons or less are not considered pets.

9. LANDLORD'S ENTRY. Except in an emergency, Landlord shall give Tenant at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice before entering the Premises. Entry shall occur between 8 in the morning and 7 in the evening.

10. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall promptly report any needed repairs to Landlord in writing. Landlord shall keep the heating, plumbing and electrical systems in good working order.

11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without the prior written consent of Landlord.

12. EARLY TERMINATION FOR JOB RELOCATION. If Tenant's employer transfers Tenant to a work location more than fifty (50) miles from the Premises, Tenant may terminate this Lease early by giving sixty (60) days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to one (1) month's rent.

13. UTILITIES. Tenant shall pay for electricity and internet service. Landlord shall pay for water, sewer and trash collection.

14. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord shall be sent to wychwood.leasing@example.com or delivered to the leasing office. The leasing office telephone number is 555-0142.

15. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in a writing signed by both parties.
